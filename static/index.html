Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deploying local AI with TensorLink.

It depends on the models you want to run. A modest workstation with 16–32GB RAM and a mid-range NVIDIA GPU (like an RTX 3060 or 4070) handles most small-to-mid business workloads. We'll assess your existing equipment on our discovery call and recommend the most cost-effective path — often you already have hardware that qualifies.

ChatGPT sends all your data to OpenAI's servers, incurs per-token API costs that compound fast, and gives you no control over the model. With TensorLink, the model runs entirely on your hardware — your data never leaves your building. You pay once (or a flat managed fee), not per query. And we connect the AI to your actual internal knowledge base, documents, and workflows — not just a generic chat interface.

Ollama is an open-source platform that makes running LLMs on local hardware as simple as one command. It supports the latest and best open-weight models — LLaMA 3.2, Mistral, Gemma, Qwen, DeepSeek, and more. It exposes an OpenAI-compatible API, which means we can swap models without changing your application code. It's fast, stable, and free.

Tailscale is a zero-trust VPN built on WireGuard that creates a private, encrypted mesh network between all your devices. We use it so your team can securely access your on-premise AI and tools from anywhere — without opening firewall ports, maintaining a VPN server, or exposing anything to the public internet. It's how we provide remote managed IT support without needing physical access.

A base deployment (local AI + JRVS assistant + secure networking) typically takes 1–3 business days. More complex engagements with full automation pipelines, RAG knowledge bases, and multi-device managed IT take 1–2 weeks. We've never had a deployment stretch beyond 30 days.
