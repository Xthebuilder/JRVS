# JRVS Pricing

## Community — Free

You run it yourself, for yourself, for non-commercial use.

You get everything: local AI chat, knowledge base, web search, file ingestion, voice, vision, MCP tools. Nothing is hobbled. The only restriction is legal — you cannot use this version to run a business, serve clients, or generate revenue. That's what the paid tiers are for.

Good for: personal projects, learning, tinkering, home use.

---

## Professional — $149 one-time

You're using JRVS at work, or it's saving you time that you bill to someone else. This tier makes that legal and adds the features that matter for that context.

**What unlocks:**
- Send emails via Gmail (`/gmail send`)
- Create and update Google Docs and Sheets
- Run the autonomous agent scheduler (`/agent start`) — the part that works while you're not at the keyboard
- Email support from the creator
- All updates for 6 months

**Who it's for:** A solo operator, consultant, or ops lead who needs JRVS handling real work — drafting emails, writing docs, running morning digests — not just answering questions.

One payment. No subscription. If you're getting even two hours a week back from the scheduler alone, this pays for itself in a month.

---

## Team — $499/month

You want the creator involved. You have a specific use case, a compliance requirement, or you need JRVS shaped to your workflow rather than the other way around.

**What's included:**
- Everything in Professional
- Monthly 30-minute call with Xavier (the person who built this)
- Custom goal configuration — Xavier sets up your `goals.yaml` for your actual inbox, your actual calendar, your actual reporting needs
- Priority support with a real response time

**Who it's for:** A small team in healthcare, legal, or financial services where the AI needs to be set up correctly, not just installed. You're paying for the creator's time, not just the software.

## Questions

**Can I try before I buy?**
Yes. Community is fully functional. Run it. If you want to send emails or start the scheduler, that's when you upgrade.

**What happens when my 6 months of updates run out on Professional?**
JRVS keeps working. You just don't get new features automatically. Renew anytime.

**Do you offer refunds?**
If it doesn't work on your setup and we can't fix it over email, yes.

**I'm a freelancer — which tier?**
Professional. You're billing clients, so Community's non-commercial restriction applies.

**My company has 50 people — do I need Team?**
Not necessarily. JRVS is currently a single-operator tool. One person runs it; everyone benefits from what it produces (the Docs it writes, the digests it sends). If you need it installed for multiple users independently, that's a Team conversation.
